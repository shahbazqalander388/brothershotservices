BROTHERS HOTSHOT SERVICES - OFFICIAL BARCODE & QR CODE ASSETS
==============================================================

All production barcodes and QR codes have been generated using verified metadata from Brothers Hotshot Services (Red Deer, AB).

1. HOW TO VIEW & DOWNLOAD (EASIEST METHOD):
   - Open your project folder: "d:\brothershotshot"
   - Double-click the file: "Brothers_Hotshot_Barcodes.html"
   - It will open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
   - You will see 4 production codes:
     * 1. Official Website QR Code (https://brothershotshot.ca/)
     * 2. Digital Contact Card vCard (Instant smartphone contact save)
     * 3. 24/7 Dispatch Direct Call (+1-587-377-0880)
     * 4. Logistics 1D Code-128 Barcode (Cargo BOL & Equipment tags)
   - Click "Download PNG (HD)" under any barcode to export a 2000px Ultra-HD image.
   - Click "Download SVG" for scalable vector graphic format.

2. PRINTING & PDF EXPORT:
   - Click "Print / Save as PDF" at the top right of the page to export an official branded presentation sheet to share with clients.

3. PRE-GENERATED VECTOR FILE:
   - "barcodes\logistics_barcode_128.svg" is already saved in this folder for vector design and printing press use.
